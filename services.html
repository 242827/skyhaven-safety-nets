Detailed service guide

Understand the solution before you choose it

Every opening, fixing surface and safety concern is different. These guides explain what each system is intended to do and what should be confirmed during a site visit.

02

Balcony Safety Nets

A made-to-measure mesh barrier designed to reduce the risk of accidental falls and objects passing through open balcony areas while retaining light and airflow.

Ask about balcony nets →

Suitable for

Apartment balconies, villa terraces, balcony rail gaps and other exposed residential openings.

What we assess

Opening dimensions, railing height, wall and ceiling surfaces, access for installation, drainage points and any building-association requirements.

Your quote should state

Net material, mesh size, twine specification, colour, perimeter rope, hook or anchor type, installation area and written warranty terms.

Care after installation

Visually inspect the mesh, knots and perimeter fixings. Keep sharp objects and heat sources away, and arrange an inspection after renovation or visible damage.

03

Children & Pet Safety

Protection planned around how children or pets use the space—not simply around its measurements. The solution may involve netting, an invisible grill or a combination of barriers.

Discuss your home →

Suitable for

Balconies, windows, staircase voids, duplex openings, terraces and indoor areas with potentially unsafe gaps.

What we assess

The user’s age or pet size, climbable furniture, railing gaps, opening direction, reachable edges and any escape or entanglement risks.

Important limitation

A safety barrier is an additional precaution. It does not replace adult supervision, responsible use, compliant railings or building-safety rules.

Handover

The completed perimeter, access points and fixings should be checked with you, together with clear cleaning, inspection and damage-reporting guidance.

04

Pigeon & Bird Control

A humane exclusion approach that blocks birds from entering or nesting in selected spaces without trapping them inside the protected area.

Request a bird-control visit →

Suitable for

Balconies, utility areas, ducts, ledges, terraces, building recesses and other recurring roosting or nesting locations.

What we assess

Bird entry routes, ledges, neighbouring openings, existing nests, cleaning access, drainage, air-conditioning units and the full perimeter requiring closure.

Installation priority

Edges and small entry points matter. The protected area should be checked carefully so birds cannot enter behind the net or become enclosed during installation.

Maintenance

Keep the area clean, inspect perimeter gaps and repair damage early. Wildlife, active nests and contaminated areas may require specialist advice before work begins.

Featured service

Invisible Grills

A slim coated stainless-steel cable system for customers who want a structured safety barrier without making the space feel enclosed. Each system is measured for the opening and fixed to a suitable track or supporting frame.

Request an invisible-grill visit →

Suitable for

Balconies, windows and selected open areas where appearance, ventilation and a more rigid cable barrier are priorities.

What we assess

Opening span, fixing surfaces, frame support, cable direction, access, railing arrangement and the appropriate spacing for the intended use.

Your quote should state

Stainless-steel grade, cable construction and diameter, protective coating, spacing, track or frame specification, fasteners and warranty conditions.

Care after installation

Check cable tension, coating, tracks and fasteners periodically. Report loose cables, damaged coating or movement at fixing points instead of adjusting them yourself.

05

Duct & Staircase Safety

Custom protection for irregular voids and internal openings where standard balcony layouts do not apply. Measurement and edge detailing are especially important.

Arrange an assessment →

Suitable for

Staircase wells, duplex voids, ventilation ducts, utility shafts, corridor openings and awkward gaps around residential or commercial buildings.

What we assess

Void geometry, fall risk, fixing access, wall condition, pipework, service access, fire or ventilation requirements and whether a removable section is needed.

Planning matters

The barrier must not obstruct essential maintenance, ventilation or required access. Any removable area should have a clearly explained fastening method.

Final inspection

We check the entire perimeter, corners, changes in direction and nearby surfaces that could damage or place additional load on the system.

06

Sports & Practice Nets

Containment netting designed around the activity, ball size, impact direction and available space. The goal is controlled practice with practical access and visibility.

Plan a practice area →

Suitable for

Cricket practice lanes, rooftop practice areas, schools, academies, gardens and selected indoor or outdoor sports spaces.

What we assess

Activity type, ball size and speed, expected impact zones, total height and length, support structure, weather exposure, gates and surrounding property.

Your quote should state

Net material, mesh and twine specification, dimensions, support arrangement, edge finishing, access opening, installation scope and maintenance terms.

Care after installation

Inspect high-impact zones, seams, supports and ground edges regularly. Usage intensity and outdoor exposure determine how often a professional inspection is sensible.
